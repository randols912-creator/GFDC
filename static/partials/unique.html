How it works: The calculator counts how many unique profiles surround a starting profile on the World Family Tree, one “step” at a time. Step 1 is the immediate family (parents, siblings, spouse/partner, children); step 2 adds their immediate families, and so on outward. Nobody is ever counted twice.

Runs of 1–3 steps finish right away on this page. Runs of 4–10 steps are handed to a background worker — you'll get an email after each step as it works (large trees can take hours, so the early emails let you watch it grow).
